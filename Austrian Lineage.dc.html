Rothbard Capital

Rothbard Capital is named after Murray Rothbard, one of the godfathers of the Austrian School of Economics. Like those before and after him, Rothbard had a decisive appreciation of entrepreneurs as the risk bearers and innovators of society. Rothbard exists to invest in and bless those entrepreneurs.
